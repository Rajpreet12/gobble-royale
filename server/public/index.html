How to win

  • 👨‍🍳 The Chef chases the nearest turkey. More Chefs arrive as the round goes on, and they get faster.
  • 🔥 The oven closes in. Stay inside the ring; a turkey left outside too long gets roasted.
  • 🌾 Hay bales block the Chef. Run circles around them.
  • 🪶 Flap to escape at the last second. It needs a moment to recharge.
  • 🤖 Not many people online? Bot turkeys fill the field so every round is a battle.
